Hello, I’m Divya!

I’m an HCI student, UX researcher, and web designer with a background in Computer Science. I’m passionate about understanding how people interact with technology and designing experiences that feel intuitive, accessible, and meaningful.

I started my journey in tech through computer science, where I built a strong foundation in problem-solving and analytical thinking. Over time, I found myself drawn to the human side of technology-how users think, behave, and experience digital products-which led me into UX design.

I’ve worked as a UI/UX design intern at Excelencia, where I gained hands-on experience in user research, wireframing, and designing user-centered solutions.

I’m currently pursuing my Master’s degree in Human-Computer Interaction at DePaul University, where I continue to strengthen my skills in UX research and interaction design.

I enjoy simplifying complex problems and creating experiences that not only look good, but work seamlessly for users.
