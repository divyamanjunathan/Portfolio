Project Overview

FocusMotion reimagines fitness by embedding physical activity into everyday travel. Instead of treating exercise as a separate task, the app encourages walking, healthier routes, and awareness of movement. I collaborated with my team on designing the interface, refining interactions, and ensuring a seamless experience across both mobile and smartwatch platforms.

Impact

Helped users see and track their daily movement, promoting healthier commuting habits and raising awareness of activity levels through engaging, habit-forming experiences.

Problem

Many students and young commuters struggle to stay active during busy routines because exercise often feels disconnected from daily life. Traditional fitness apps demand extra time and effort, which leads to low engagement and inconsistent habits.

Solution

FocusMotion integrates movement into everyday commuting by suggesting active routes, micro wellness goals, and seamless mobile + smartwatch tracking. The experience makes physical activity feel natural, accessible, and sustainable rather than like a separate task.

Reflection

What I Learned

This project reinforced the importance of designing for habit formation and integrating wellness into daily life. I learned how to translate research into actionable UX solutions while balancing simplicity, motivation, and functionality.

Designing for both mobile and smartwatch platforms taught me to prioritize context-aware experiences and maintain strong visual and interaction consistency across devices. Collaborating on branding, prototyping, and user testing also strengthened my communication and teamwork skills throughout the process.
